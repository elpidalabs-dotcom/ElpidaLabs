THE PRODUCT DIRECTION

From a token launch
to a lasting community.

Launching is a moment. Building something people can understand and participate in is a longer journey.

Elpida is exploring a connected experience for creation, discovery, and community growth across chains. The aim is to make the journey easier to understand without hiding the complexity and responsibility that come with onchain participation.

01 / PROPOSED DIRECTION

Create

Investigate the steps, information, and controls a creator would need to prepare a token launch.

02 / PROPOSED DIRECTION

Discover

Explore how people could understand projects, compare context, and find communities across supported networks.

03 / PROPOSED DIRECTION

Grow

Consider what creators and communities need after launch, including clearer project information and ongoing communication.
