What is Elpida?

Elpida is a multichain token launchpad built and operated by Elpida Labs. It brings token creation, project discovery, and related onchain tools together at elpida.fun.

The product is live on mainnet. Its focus is the journey from preparing a token and its project information to reviewing the launch and finding projects through a shared explorer.

How token creation works

The live creation flow is organized into five stages:

  1. Token details. Add the name, symbol, description, logo, and optional community links that describe a project.
  2. Trading pair. Choose from the pairing options available on the selected network.
  3. Creator rewards. Review and configure the creator-reward details presented by the product.
  4. Optional creator purchase. Review an initial purchase when that option is available.
  5. Review and create. Inspect the network, launch summary, fees, and transaction details before proceeding in the live app.

The creation page includes a project preview and launch summary. Current options and amounts are shown by the live product rather than fixed in this article.

Discovery and the wider product

The explorer helps visitors find indexed launches. It supports filtering by a token’s name, ticker, or address, with chain and pairing filters and several sorting views.

Elpida also provides destinations for portfolio, creator rewards, swap, and analytics. These views serve different parts of the product journey; their current data, network coverage, and account requirements are shown in the app.

Mainnet networks

On 25 November 2026, Elpida’s network selector listed Base, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, and Ethereum as Mainnet options. The public status endpoint reported all four configurations as enabled at the time of review.

Network availability and individual operations can change. The selected network, live status, and transaction review in elpida.fun provide the current details.

A live product that keeps evolving

Mainnet is Elpida’s current deployment stage. Development continues as the team improves the product and documents its capabilities. Future features and additional network coverage will be described as they become available.

This article is an introduction to the product. For current operating details, refer to the live app, its terms, and its risk information.
