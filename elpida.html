THE PRODUCT DIRECTION

From a token launch
to a lasting community.

Launching is a moment. Building something people can understand and participate in is a longer journey.

Elpida now runs on mainnet. Its live interface brings token details, trading-pair selection, creator rewards, an optional creator purchase, and a review step into the creation journey. Visit the product for current network availability, fees, and transaction details.

AN HONEST DEVELOPMENT NOTE

Ambition, with
the status attached.

Live product, continuing development

Elpida is live on mainnet at elpida.fun. On 25 November 2026, the live site displayed Base Mainnet and a token-creation flow. Its network selector also listed BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, and Ethereum as Mainnet options. A listed network is not a claim that every operation is available on it; check the product’s current state before proceeding.

The interactive study on this company site remains an illustrative demo. Actual token creation, wallet connections, fees, and transactions take place on elpida.fun. Mainnet availability does not establish an audit or guarantee outcomes.
